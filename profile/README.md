# masUNO

**"Can I join?" becomes "We're looking for you."**

*Más uno* means "plus one." masUNO connects students who want to play with groups that are one or two players short.

[Website](https://masuno-app.github.io) · [iOS app](https://github.com/masUNO-app/ios)

</div>

---

## The problem

On every campus there's someone who wants to play but won't walk up to strangers, and a group across the quad that's one player short. They need each other. Neither knows it.

We think this is less a social problem than a coordination problem. Students don't need an icebreaker. They need a reason to show up: a spot that's open and a group that wants them there.

## How it works

- **See what's nearby.** The home screen is a map of games happening around campus, with distance, walking time and spots left.
- **Join in seconds.** Browse without an account. Verify with your student email only when you join.
- **Know who's going.** Host and attendee faces come before logistics, and you can bring a friend.
- **Post a game in under a minute.** Hosts list what, when, where and how many players they need.
- **Spots that come back.** A full game leaves the map. If someone drops out, it reappears and everyone who bookmarked it gets notified.

We're starting with sports, then expanding to club events and other activities.
